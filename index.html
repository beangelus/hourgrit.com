UnisOrder

Unified order operations for Shopee & Lazada

Process orders from Shopee and Lazada in one list, print shipping labels in Korean, and manage inventory and purchasing in the same place.

  • Orders from 8 countries collected into one list
  • Batch processing of multiple orders at once
  • Shipping labels with product names in Korean
  • Low stock added to the purchase list automatically
  • Sales, margin and inventory insights on one dashboard
